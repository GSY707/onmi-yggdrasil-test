正式 C0 已完成，明确记录 `fresh_initialization=true`，未加载 `c0-overfit32` checkpoint。结果如下：

| split | state full exact | final answer accuracy | source pointer | target pointer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ordinary test | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| length heldout | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| relation heldout | 0.765625 | 0.93359375 | 1.0 | 1.0 |

后续只读 checkpoint 诊断显示：test/length/relation/causal 的 oracle-reset one-step accuracy 和 predicted hard re-embed diagnostic trajectory full 都为 `1.0`；relation 首次错误从第 2 步开始。失败归因为连续 latent closure，而不是 pointer 或单步 operator。诊断见 `artifacts/v2-a/a1_6/diagnostics/closure-diagnostic.json`。
